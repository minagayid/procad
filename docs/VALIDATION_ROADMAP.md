# Dental CAD/CAM implementation and validation roadmap

Research review: 2026-09-27. This roadmap separates deterministic software improvements from evidence needed to claim a clinically or manufacturably validated product. Public research and machine specifications are design inputs; they do not validate procad.

## Research findings that shape the design

- **Margin identification needs labelled cases and review.** A recent 3D mesh segmentation study reports 1,113 prepared-tooth meshes, with 78 held-out cases; 70/78 and 62/78 outputs were within that paper's 200 μm and 100 μm maximum-distance cutoffs from technician/crown-bottom-derived reference labels. These are study-specific label-distance counts, not clinical correctness, a universal tolerance, or a procad result. External scanner/site representation, label agreement, patient-level split details, and dataset reuse rights remain unconfirmed. [Guibault et al., 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010482525013125)
- **Digital fit needs a physical reference.** A systematic review compares cross-section, silicone replica, micro-CT, and triple-scan methods. Triple scan acquires the preparation, the restoration intaglio, and the assembled restoration; micro-CT and replica methods provide independent alternatives with different cost, access, and measurement limitations. A geometric offset rendered by CAD is not a measurement of fabricated fit. [Systematic review of fit-assessment methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10419640/)
- **A bite transform needs repeatability and trueness evidence.** A randomized clinical study evaluates repeated virtual interocclusal records, but its in-vivo design could assess precision and not trueness against a known pose. For a buccal bite scan with two arch patches, estimate two transforms into the common bite frame; residuals and held-out coverage are diagnostics, not trueness or uncertainty. [RCT on static interocclusal registration](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9713976/)
- **Implant libraries are not interchangeable geometry.** A systematic review reports heterogeneous evidence on scan-body geometry and workflow factors; it does not establish one optimal design or universal scanner/design relationship. Require exact manufacturer/system, connection, scan-body and Ti-base identifiers, and library checksum/version; reject unknown or mismatched components instead of fitting a generic cylinder. [Scan-body design systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37771200/)
- **Bridge connectors are indication and material dependent.** A recent review focuses on zirconia connector/span evidence, including in-vitro and finite-element work; broader evidence does not establish one universal connector size or show that simulation alone proves clinical durability. Store cross-section measurements and material-specific instructions; require physical and expert evidence for claims. [Connector and span systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42645474/), [systematic review of tooth-supported FDP biomechanics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13363059/)
- **A CAM database entry is not a qualified machine/material route.** DGSHAPE's updater page listed CAM 2025 V25.1.7 on 2026-05-29; the cited V25.1.0 guide is historical. That guide shows a VITA SUPRINITY PC `LS-14` entry, while VITA's current product page calls the physical blank `PC-14`, with different listed dimensions and a system-partner list naming DWX-4W rather than DWX-43W. Keep these records separate and require manufacturer/lab confirmation of the actual package, holder, material entry, and machine definition. DGSHAPE also documents a kinematic simulation and explicitly warns that interrupted simulations leave unrun collisions unchecked. [DGSHAPE version history](https://downloadcenter.rolanddg.com/softwareparts.php?id=dadd2c5ebadf868baa36f3287b3e2363ce64a6ab&item_id=108c72d8fd63d19dc030f6f6bc0e0bde72c3c4c2&software=1f14761f400e6f7c8696a594d63e6dfa60dfd553&type=note_history_html), [VITA material listing](https://www.vita-zahnfabrik.com/pdb_ccdc92_en.html_us), [DGSHAPE simulation help](https://downloadcenter.rolanddg.com/contents/manuals/DGSHAPE_CAM_for_DWX-43W_USE_EN/cei1731550195730.html)
- **Accuracy, conformity, and software quality have separate standards.** ISO 20896-1:2019 addresses handheld intraoral-scan accuracy (reconfirmed current in 2025); ISO/TR 20896-2:2023 describes accuracy methods for implanted-device scans; ISO 5725-1:2023 defines trueness and precision; ISO 14253-1:2017 describes conformity decisions that account for measurement uncertainty. ISO 23298:2023 addresses milling-machine machining-accuracy test methods. ISO 6872:2024 addresses dental ceramic material requirements/tests; an ISO/CD 6872 revision is under development as of this research snapshot. ISO 14971:2019, IEC 62304:2006+A1:2015, IEC 62366-1:2015+A1:2020, and ISO 13485:2016 are relevant risk, software lifecycle, usability, and QMS process anchors. None certifies procad, a restoration, or a complete CAM route; applicability depends on intended use and jurisdiction. [ISO 20896-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/69402.html), [ISO/TR 20896-2](https://committee.iso.org/standard/76858.html), [ISO 5725-1](https://committee.iso.org/cms/live/live/en/sites/isoorg/contents/data/standard/06/94/69418.html?browse=ics), [ISO 14253-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/70137.html), [ISO 23298](https://www.iso.org/standard/75167.html), [ISO 6872:2024](https://committee.iso.org/standard/81718.html), [ISO/CD 6872](https://www.iso.org/standard/91139.html), [ISO 14971](https://www.iso.org/standard/72704.html), [IEC 62304](https://www.iso.org/standard/38421.html), [IEC 62366-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/63179.html), [ISO 13485](https://www.iso.org/standard/59752.html)

## Workflow solutions and current state

| Margin between picked points | Connect operator points over the preparation mesh. The app uses an A* shortest path on a welded triangle-edge graph with the mesh validator's weld tolerance, projects picks to the source surface, and accepts open scan patches. Keep raw clicks, source hash, algorithm version, and derived path distinct. | Implemented as an operator-guided faceted path. Input is capped at 150,000 source positions and 450,000 triangle indices for bounded graph memory. Sparse clicks can still select an unintended shortest route. Preview construction remains synchronous and requires a star-shaped projection in prep-local XY. It does not identify the clinical finish line or infer through hidden/defective scan regions. | Experts independently label circumferential finish lines on rights-cleared scans. Predeclare distance, continuity, coverage, and failure metrics; report per-tooth and per-scanner strata, ambiguous/hidden regions, inter-rater agreement, and independent holdout performance. No auto-accept of uncertain segments.
| Intaglio fit | Sample the preparation surface and use outward normal rays. Require the first positive hit on the final crown to be opposed-facing and agree with the first same-facing hit on the exact Boolean subtraction surface; otherwise mark the sample unmatched. Keep this result distinct from fabricated-part metrology. | Implemented as a CAD-only Boolean/crown boundary consistency diagnostic; a synthetic Boolean shell with a known 1 mm gap checks measured hits, intentionally open regions, rigid transforms, and rejection of a nearer non-intaglio surface. It does not measure fabricated fit, penetration where unmatched, seating, uncertainty, or clinical acceptance. | Validate the sampling, region rule, and uncertainty on calibrated known-gap artefacts; then use a fit-for-purpose reference chain on fabricated cases (e.g. triple-scan, replica, or micro-CT as justified). ## Implementation stages

0. **Intended use and risk contract:** name the first supported user, indication, jurisdiction, input/acquisition methods, outputs and prohibited claims. Define coordinate-frame, unit, source-integrity, hazard, usability and verification records before adding clinical release gates.
1. **Geometry foundation (current increment):** connect operator points with a source-surface mesh-edge path; share the mesh weld tolerance; bound graph memory; preserve source-bound clicks and algorithm version. Next improve route intent and replace XY radial preview reconstruction with a contour-preserving surface loop. Keep the annotation operator-authored and clinically unverified.
2. **Metrology prototype:** distinguish the CAD design intaglio from fabricated restoration metrology. Start with a fit-for-purpose calibrated/traceable reference chain and uncertainty budget; assess analytic known-gap artefacts, then fabricated parts using a suitable reference such as triple-scan, replica, or micro-CT as justified. Do not require every method on every case or label a result fit merely because it is signed.
3. **Registration prototype:** create a versioned bite-record entity with two separately registered arch patches, robust correspondence, held-out coverage/residual checks, proper rigid transforms and full provenance. The first output may be static unsigned geometric proximity only. Do not call zero-distance samples contacts or enable dynamic/force claims.
4. **Restoration modules:** separate crown, bridge, and implant domain objects. Implement shared bridge insertion/accessibility and named connector cross-section measurements; add implant interfaces only from exact licensed libraries. Keep unsupported indications rejected.
5. **Manufacturing route:** keep the public candidate profile blocked until an owner supplies exact machine, CAM, fixture, tool and material-lot data. Integrate with the documented licensed CAM route instead of emitting invented machine code. Add simulation and physical test-coupon evidence before allowing even a qualified route.
6. **Validation and release:** secure scan/component/material rights; maintain patient/acquisition-separated development and locked test sets; predeclare criteria with clinical, lab, metrology and regulatory experts; run software, bench, usability and applicable clinical validation; maintain change-control and post-market evidence.

## Current technical checks

The current software tests analytic mesh-distance cases, invalid and self-touching annotation loops, reflected transforms, and a curved sphere mesh whose sampled margin path is checked against the source surface. These tests show code behavior on synthetic geometry; they do not validate clinical margin identification, physical fit, bite registration, bridge strength, implant interface compatibility, CAM simulation, or manufactured restorations.
