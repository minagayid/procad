**Modifications by OrganUI (2026):** selected 33 meshes, triangulated polygon faces, recentered and uniformly scaled coordinates, transformed axes from Z-up to Y-up using `[x, z, -y]`, recomputed smooth normals, assigned illustrative colors and converted OBJ to GLB. No decimation or invented anatomy. procad imports the pinned GLB unchanged.

**Coverage limits:** 28 permanent teeth, two gingiva structures and three jaw-bone structures. It has no third molars (wisdom teeth), no primary teeth, and no separated enamel/dentine/pulp layers. It is an educational anatomy reference; expert anatomical review is pending. The source notes that the database can contain missing or incorrect anatomical representations. Do not use it as a patient-specific or clinically validated tooth library.
