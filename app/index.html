Supported scan formats

Surface meshSTL · PLY · OBJ · OFF
Point cloudXYZ · PTS · CSV · ASCII PCD
Review file handoffBinary STL or OBJ with millimeter coordinates

The application preserves source provenance and does not guess units. Point clouds can be inspected and prepared for registration, but they are not used for Boolean crown generation until converted to a validated surface mesh. DICOM, 3MF, GLB, and proprietary scanner formats require a documented external conversion step.

Abutment design is currently a professional brief only; manufacturing geometry is blocked until a validated abutment algorithm and review protocol are added.
